# JOMP Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 — August 2026

Source: official JOMP Brand Kit (docx v1.0). This is the locked identity for product UI.

## Brand line

Connecting Talent. Creating Opportunities.

## Attributes

- Professional — institutional, not casual
- Structured — monogram from clean geometric strokes
- Singular palette — one navy, used with intent
- Human-centered — people, connection, upward movement

## Logo system

One monogram (the JP mark) in seven configurations:

1. Primary lockup — monogram, divider rule, wordmark, tagline (default)
2. Primary monogram — JP mark standalone
3. Horizontal — monogram + wordmark on one baseline
4. Stacked — monogram above wordmark
5. Icon only — rounded-square app-icon crop
6. Navy on white — default on light backgrounds
7. White on navy — reversed on dark backgrounds

Construction is fixed. Do not redraw, re-angle, or simplify the J/P overlap. Keep clear space equal to the height of the monogram head. Do not stretch, thicken, or remove the divider in the primary lockup.

### Minimum size

| Use | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Digital — full lockup | 120px wide |
| Digital — monogram / icon | 32px wide |
| Print — full lockup | 25mm wide |
| Print — monogram only | 10mm wide |

## Color palette

Do not introduce secondary accent colors.

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Navy | `#01224F` | 1, 34, 79 | Logo, wordmark, primary UI, headlines on white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Background, reversed logo and type on navy |
| Black | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 | Body copy fallback only when navy fails contrast |

`#01224F` is locked. Do not drift toward a lighter corporate blue.

## Typography

Wordmark and tagline: bold geometric sans, all caps. Tagline uses wider tracking than the wordmark.

Recommended pairing: Poppins / Montserrat.

- Display / wordmark — Bold–Black, all caps, tight tracking
- Tagline — Medium, all caps, tracking +40 to +60
- Body — Regular/Medium grotesk (Inter, Arial, Helvetica)

## Concept anchors

- Connection — the link between employers and job seekers
- People — the circular head on the monogram
- Opportunity — upward movement / growth

## Do not

- Recolor the monogram outside navy-on-white or white-on-navy
- Separate the monogram from its circular head
- Condense, expand, or italicize the wordmark
- Place the primary lockup on busy photography without a solid navy or white field
- Shrink lockups below minimum size
- Introduce a secondary accent color into the logo system
- Rebuild the divider at a different weight or length
